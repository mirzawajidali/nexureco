Refund Policy

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, you can return or exchange it within 7 days of delivery.

What can be returned

  • Items returned within 7 days of the delivery date
  • Items that are unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging
  • Items that arrived damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered (please include a photo when you contact us)

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Contact us at support@nexureco.com or +92 316 7936302 (Mon - Sat, 10am - 8pm PKT) with your order number and the item you want to return or exchange.
  2. We'll confirm your return and share the return address.
