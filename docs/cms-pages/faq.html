Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days anywhere in Pakistan.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is Rs. 200, and free on orders of Rs. 5,000 or more.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept Cash on Delivery (COD) nationwide.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes! We offer a 7-day return policy. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging. Exchanges for a different size or colour are subject to availability. See our Refund Policy for details.

How do I track my order?

Enter your order number on the Track Order page. If you have an account, you can also see your orders in your account dashboard.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only ship within Pakistan.
