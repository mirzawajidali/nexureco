Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Nexure CO ("we", "us") collects when you visit nexureco.com or buy from us, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Orders: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address, and the items you buy. We use these to deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • Account: your name, email address, phone number and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form), your saved addresses, order history and wishlist.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Contact form, emails and calls: the details and messages you send us.
  • Reviews: your review, rating, any photos you add and the name you choose to show. Reviews and their photos are public. To review without an account you enter your order number and the email or phone you ordered with; we only use these to check the purchase.
  • Unfinished checkouts: if you enter your details at checkout but don't place the order, we save your bag, name, phone number, email and city, so we can send you a reminder on WhatsApp with a link back to your bag.
  • Chat assistant: the messages you type into the chat on our website.

We only accept Cash on Delivery, so we never collect or store card or bank details through our website.

Information collected automatically

When you browse our website, we and our analytics and advertising partners collect information about your device and how you use the site. This includes your IP address, browser and device type, the pages and products you view, items you add to your bag, purchases, and the website that referred you.

Analytics, advertising pixels and cookies

We use the following tools. They set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google): to understand how visitors use our site. If you are signed in, we send your account's internal ID number (not your name or email) so visits from your different devices are counted as one visitor.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram): to measure our ads and show our ads to people who have visited our site.
  • TikTok Pixel (TikTok): to measure our TikTok ads and show our ads to people who have visited our site.

These tools record which pages and products you view and what you add to your bag or buy. We do not send them your name, email address or phone number.

Our website also stores your shopping bag, wishlist, recently viewed products and sign-in session in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you come back. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can control ad personalisation in your Google, Facebook/Instagram and TikTok account settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns and exchanges, including asking you on WhatsApp or by phone to confirm a cash on delivery order before we send it
  • To remind you on WhatsApp about a checkout you didn't finish, and to ask for a review once your order is delivered
  • To run your account
  • To answer your questions through our contact form, email, phone or chat assistant
  • To send you our newsletter and offers, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it
  • To measure and show our ads on Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website secure

Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners: your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your order and collect payment.
  • Google, Meta and TikTok: the browsing and purchase information described above.
  • Google (Gemini): messages you type into our chat assistant are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate replies. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.
  • Service providers: our website hosting, content delivery and security provider (Cloudflare), and our email provider. They process data only to run our services.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: as long as needed to handle returns and to meet our accounting, tax and legal obligations
  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics and advertising data: according to Google's, Meta's and TikTok's own retention settings

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your personal information, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to marketing at any time. Email support@nexureco.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We may need to keep some order information when the law requires it.

Security

Our website uses HTTPS encryption, passwords are stored hashed, and access to customer data is limited to staff who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Our website is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
