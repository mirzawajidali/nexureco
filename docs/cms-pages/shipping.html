Shipping Policy

We deliver nationwide across Pakistan. All orders are Cash on Delivery: you pay when your parcel arrives.

  • Standard Delivery: 3-5 business days, Rs. 200
  • Free Shipping: on all orders of Rs. 5,000 or more

Delivery times can be longer during sales and public holidays. Once your order ships you can follow it on the Track Order page.

Returns & Exchanges

Not satisfied? No worries!

  • 7-day return policy from the date of delivery
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item
  • Exchanges are available for a different size or colour (subject to availability)

See our Refund Policy for how to start a return.
